# SunVolt Solar - Company overview
SunVolt Solar is a (fictional) rooftop solar installer serving homes and small businesses in Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune and Mumbai. We handle survey, design, net-metering paperwork, installation and 25 years of support.

# Home plans and pricing
- SunVolt Home 3 kW: Rs 1,80,000 before subsidy. Suits monthly electricity bills of Rs 2,000 to Rs 3,500. Generates about 360 units per month.
- SunVolt Home 5 kW: Rs 2,90,000 before subsidy. Suits monthly bills of Rs 3,500 to Rs 6,000. Generates about 600 units per month.
- SunVolt Home 10 kW: Rs 5,50,000. Suits bills above Rs 6,000 or homes with EV charging.
Prices include panels, inverter, mounting structure, wiring, installation and net-metering application.

# Government subsidy
Residential customers can claim the central rooftop solar subsidy (PM Surya Ghar): Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW and Rs 78,000 for 3 kW and above. SunVolt files the subsidy application for the customer. The subsidy is credited to the customer's bank account after commissioning, usually within 30 to 45 days. Subsidy applies only to residential homes, not commercial property.

# Savings and payback
A typical home saves 80 to 95 percent of its electricity bill. Payback period is usually 3.5 to 5 years after subsidy. Panels last 25+ years.

# EMI and financing
Zero-cost EMI is available for 12 months through partner banks. Longer EMI of up to 60 months is available at around 9.5 to 11 percent interest. Monthly EMI is often lower than the current electricity bill.

# Commercial plans
For shops, offices and factories we design 10 kW to 500 kW systems. Pricing is about Rs 45,000 to Rs 50,000 per kW. Commercial customers get accelerated depreciation tax benefits but no subsidy.
