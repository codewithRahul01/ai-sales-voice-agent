# Free site survey
The first step is a free site survey. An engineer visits, checks roof area, shading and structure, and gives an exact quote. It takes about 45 minutes. There is no obligation to buy.

# Roof requirements
About 100 square feet of shadow-free roof is needed per kW. Flat concrete roofs and metal sheet roofs both work. Apartment residents need society (RWA) permission for the common terrace; SunVolt helps with the paperwork.

# Installation timeline
Installation takes 1 to 2 days after design approval. Net-metering approval from the electricity board takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on the city.

# Warranty and maintenance
Panels carry a 25-year performance warranty and the inverter a 7 to 10 year warranty. SunVolt includes 5 years of free maintenance with 2 cleanings per year. The app shows live generation.

# Objection: it is too expensive
With subsidy and EMI, most customers pay less per month in EMI than their current bill, and after payback the electricity is nearly free for 20 years.

# Objection: what about monsoon and cloudy days
Panels still produce 20 to 40 percent on cloudy days. Net metering stores excess units from sunny months as credits, so the yearly saving stays high.

# Objection: I am renting
The decision usually needs the property owner. We can share a brochure for the owner or schedule a call with them.

# Objection: I need to think or discuss with family
That is completely fine. The free site survey gives an exact quote to discuss with family, with no obligation.
